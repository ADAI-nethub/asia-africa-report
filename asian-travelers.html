"The most important distinction is not Chinese vs Indian vs Japanese—it is which Africa they believe they are visiting."
Moving beyond nationality and into psychometrics—motivations, risk tolerance, status signaling, novelty-seeking, spending behavior, identity, and travel narratives—the Asian traveler to Africa is not one market but several distinct psychological tribes.

The mental model a traveler holds often predicts behavior more accurately than passport of origin. For the Great Green Wall states specifically, visitors are far more likely to be motivated by commerce, networks, religion, infrastructure, and long-term positioning than by classic safari-tourism drivers.
